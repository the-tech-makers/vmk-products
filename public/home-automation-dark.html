Knowledge Base

Inquiries, clarified.

What systems can be automated in my residence? +
VMK Products engineers complete residential automation including full-spectrum circadian lighting, motorized drapery and roman blinds, multi-zone VRV/VRF air conditioning, private Dolby Atmos cinema screening rooms, luxury Italian touch modular switches, biometric deadbolts, and 24/7 video security surveillance.
Can I automate an already constructed home without trenching walls? +
Yes, absolutely. We specialize in wireless non-invasive retrofitting. Our ultra-compact micro-modules fit invisibly behind your existing electrical switch boxes and our motorized drapery tracks install without breaking plaster, chipping brickwork, or damaging existing interior woodwork.
What happens if the home internet broadband goes offline? +
Your home continues to function perfectly. All physical switch plates, presence radar triggers, scheduled circadian curves, and local scene activations communicate directly over a localized edge mesh hub with 12ms latency. Only remote controls outside your home require internet connectivity.
Can we automate room-by-room or start with a single floor? +
Yes. Our modular architecture allows you to start with high-impact zones—such as the Grand Living salon, Master Suite motorized drapery, or Home Cinema—and effortlessly expand to other rooms over time without needing to replace core equipment.
What areas across Delhi NCR does VMK Products service? +
We provide full on-site consultation, architectural engineering, installation, and after-sales service throughout Delhi, Greater Noida, Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad, and Faridabad.
