GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

What can I automate in my home?+

Lighting, curtains, security, climate control, entertainment, selected appliances and more. The right setup depends on your space and requirements.

Can I automate my existing home?+

Many solutions can be added to existing homes. VMK can assess your electrical setup and recommend suitable options.

Do I need to automate the whole house at once?+

No. You can begin with the areas that matter most and plan a system that can grow with your needs, subject to product compatibility.

How much does home automation cost?+

Cost depends on the size of your home, selected products, wiring and level of automation. A consultation helps us understand your requirements before recommending a solution.

Can I control my home when I'm away?+

Supported devices can offer remote app control and alerts. Available functions depend on the products, connectivity and configuration.
