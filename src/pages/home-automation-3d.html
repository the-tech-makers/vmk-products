COMMON INQUIRIES

Questions,
answered.

What can I automate in my home with VMK? +

VMK provides end-to-end automation covering architectural smart lighting, motorized curtains and sheer blinds, multi-zone air conditioning, home cinema systems, touch modular switches, biometric smart locks, and 24/7 security surveillance cameras.

Can I automate an existing built home without cutting walls? +

Yes. Many of our solutions are designed for seamless retrofitting. We install wireless smart modules directly inside your existing electrical switch boxes and install motorized curtain tracks without breaking plaster or repainting walls.

Do I need to automate the entire house all at once? +

No. Our modular system architecture allows you to begin with the zones that matter most—such as the living room, master suite, or motorized shading—and expand seamlessly over time as your requirements evolve.

Will my smart switches still work if the internet is down? +

Yes, absolutely. All physical switch presses, automated motion triggers, and pre-programmed schedules run on a local network hub. Only remote access from outside your home requires active broadband.

How much does home automation typically cost? +

Costs depend on the size of your property, number of lighting circuits, motorized windows, and selected devices. During our initial consultation, we provide a transparent, itemized proposal tailored to your floor plan and budget.

What locations does VMK Products service? +

VMK Products provides on-site consultation, engineering, installation, and after-sales support across Delhi, Greater Noida, Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad, and surrounding areas in Delhi NCR.
