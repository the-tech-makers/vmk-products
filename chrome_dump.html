Frequently asked questions

What is home automation and how does it work?

Home automation connects devices such as lights, curtains, security systems, climate control and entertainment so they can be controlled individually or together. You can use wall panels, mobile apps, schedules, sensors or supported voice assistants to control your home.

Can I automate my existing home without major renovation?

Yes. Many home automation solutions can be installed in existing homes with minimal structural changes. During the consultation, VMK Products can assess your existing electrical setup and recommend suitable retrofit solutions.

Do I need to change my existing switches to make my home smart?

It depends on the automation system and the existing wiring in your home. VMK Products can recommend smart switches, modules or other compatible solutions based on your current electrical setup.

What can I automate in my home?

Depending on your requirements, you can automate lighting, fans, curtains and blinds, air conditioning, security cameras, smart locks, gates, entertainment systems, appliances, sensors and more.

Can I control my home automation from my phone?

Yes. Compatible automation systems can be controlled through a smartphone or tablet. Depending on the products installed, you may be able to control lighting, security, curtains, climate and other connected devices remotely.

Can lighting, curtains and other devices work together automatically?

Yes. Compatible devices can be combined into scenes or routines. For example, a "Movie" scene could dim the lights, close the curtains and switch on your entertainment system with a single command.

Will home automation still work if the internet goes down?

This depends on the automation technology and products selected. Some locally controlled functions can continue working without an internet connection, while remote access and certain cloud-based features may require internet connectivity.

Can I automate security cameras, smart locks and gates?

Yes, compatible security products can be integrated into a smart-home system. Depending on the selected equipment, you can monitor cameras, manage smart locks, control gates and receive security alerts from supported devices.

Is home automation suitable for apartments and flats?

Yes. Home automation can be installed in apartments, flats, villas and independent houses. The solution can be planned around the property's existing wiring, electrical points and available space.

Is home automation suitable for a new construction project?

Yes. Planning automation during construction can make it easier to coordinate electrical wiring, control panels, sensors, motorized curtains, security and other smart-home requirements from the beginning.

How much does home automation cost?

The cost depends on the size of the property, number of rooms, number of devices, type of automation, selected products and installation requirements. VMK Products can provide a more accurate estimate after understanding your home's requirements.

Can I start with a few smart features and add more later?

Yes. You can begin with areas such as lighting, curtains or security and expand the system later, provided the selected technology and products support future integration.

Does VMK Products provide installation and setup?

Yes. VMK Products provides installation and configuration for the recommended automation solutions. The exact installation process depends on the property, products and scope of the project.

How long does it take to install a home automation system?

Installation time varies depending on the size of the property, number of devices, wiring requirements and complexity of the automation system. A project timeline can be discussed after the site assessment and system design.

Do you provide home automation solutions in Delhi and Greater Noida?

VMK Products provides home automation solutions for residential projects in Delhi, Greater Noida and surrounding areas. Contact our team to discuss your property's requirements and project location.
